Selection and Evolution with a Deck of Cards

You are one student in a class population. Draw your alleles, mate, face a selection event, and find out whether the class average changes the way the theory predicts: R = h²S. The rest of the class is simulated, using the same shuffled decks and the same rules as the classroom exercise.

Scoring: ace = 1, face cards = 12, all other cards = face value. Cards are drawn without replacement from a shuffled shoe of two or more 52-card decks (no jokers), as in the article. Heritability is the slope of offspring phenotype on mid-parent phenotype. Selection differential S = mean after selection − mean before. Response R = mean of the next generation − mean of the generation before selection. Changing any setting and starting a new class resets the run.
